Resource Calculation Rules

The Resource Planner uses the following rules to estimate the required supplies:

  • Food: 3 meals per person per day
  • Water: 5 litres per person per day
  • Energy: 10 units per person per day
  • Security Cards: 1 card per person
  • Sleeping Pods: 1 pod per person
